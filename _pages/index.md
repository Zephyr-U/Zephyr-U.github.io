Hi, I'm Chi-Wa U (Zephyr). I develop circuits and systems for biomedical sensing and monitoring.

I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the [Microelectronics Lab (meLAB)](https://melabresearch.com/), University of Glasgow. I also continue to collaborate with Prof. [Chi-Seng Lam](https://ime.um.edu.mo/people/cslam/) at the [Institute of Microelectronics (IME)](https://ime.um.edu.mo/), University of Macau, jointly guiding students in integrated circuit research.

Research interests
======

* Ultra-low-power analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits
* Precision voltage references and self-calibration techniques
* Biomedical ASICs for sensing, imaging, and implantable systems
* Ultrasonic imaging transceivers and photoacoustic imaging systems
* Power management circuits for IoT, wearable, and electric-vehicle systems

Recent News
======

* **June 30, 2026** — Two papers in *IEEE TCAS-I*, led by Haochen Yu and Yin Zhang, will be presented at ISICAS 2026.

Selected current work
=======

I am the principal investigator of an NSFC Young Scientist Fund project on self-calibration bandgap reference circuits and a co-principal investigator of a University of Macau funded project on ASIC-enhanced photoacoustic blood vessel imaging. I also contribute to research projects on ultrasonic imaging ASICs, hybrid DC-DC converters, and low-power voltage references for IoT systems.
