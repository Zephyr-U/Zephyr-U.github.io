Circuits & Systems for Biomedical Applications

My research focuses on circuits and systems for biomedical applications, including sensing and monitoring. Integrated circuit design is one enabling approach within this work, together with signal processing and system integration.

BGR Trimming and Auto-Calibration

Process variation affects both the output voltage and temperature dependence of bandgap references. This work uses on-chip automatic calibration to reduce reference error and simplify trimming in a low-power voltage reference.

Related paper: Sub-µW Auto-Calibration Bandgap Voltage Reference, IEEE JSSC

AI Signal Gating

We are investigating AI-based reliability gating for bioimpedance measurements. This ongoing research aims to retain reliable signal segments and withhold uncertain measurements when sensing conditions change.

Temperature Sensors

Temperature Sensing for Energy-Harvesting Nodes

Temperature awareness during cold start requires sensors that can operate with very limited supply voltage. This work develops an untrimmed BJT-based temperature sensor operating at 0.35 V for energy-harvesting AIoT nodes.

Related paper: A 0.35-V Untrimmed BJT-Based Temperature Sensor, IEEE TCAS-I

Compact On-Chip Temperature Sensing

Dense on-chip thermal monitoring calls for small temperature sensors that can be placed near heat sources. This work develops a 555-µm² digital temperature sensor in 65-nm CMOS for thermal management of AI processors.

Related paper: A 555-µm² Digital Temperature Sensor, IEEE TCAS-I
